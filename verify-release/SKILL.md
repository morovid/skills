# Release Verification Gate

Determine whether one exact candidate meets all applicable release conditions. Own `release-verification.md`, an index of authority, actual results, unresolved findings, and the release decision. Tie together existing reviews and execution evidence without replacing their independent judgments or approving deployment.

## 1. Identify the candidate and required conditions

Read repository instructions, the current specification/review package and applicable catalogs/decisions, implementation plan/review, test analysis and reviewed test implementation plan/category designs, and [review-code](../review-code/SKILL.md), [review-tests](../review-tests/SKILL.md), and [review-security](../review-security/SKILL.md) reports. Inspect actual build/test/CI configuration, relevant dependency locks, release/deployment definitions, applicable release policy and runbooks, and prior gate findings/responses. Equivalent artifacts suffice when they establish the same authority and detail; filenames and prior skill invocation are not gates.

Pin the candidate: exact source revision or reproducible snapshot, relevant local changes, dependency locks, build toolchain/options, relevant configuration and target environment, and immutable artifact digest/version where packaging applies. A branch name or mutable image tag alone is insufficient. Identify how source-level checks and reviews link to the built artifact and which checks must exercise that artifact. Record actual dates and environment differences; redact secrets rather than copying their values. A local snapshot can support verification but cannot be represented as a releasable artifact unless its release identity and required provenance are established.

Use [the gate rubric](references/quality-rubric.md) to build the check register before execution. For each required condition record its authority, exact command or inspection method, scope/environment, expected outcome or established threshold, dependencies, evidence, and owner. Reconcile requirements/AC/B/I/DoD, plan completion checks, test obligations, security conditions, and release policy. Conditional checks need architecture/change-based applicability reasons. Do not drop a mandatory check because no script implements it.

Use established project commands and criteria. Ground unresolved command details in repository configuration; do not invent quality scores, coverage targets, latency budgets, severity exceptions, test categories, approvals, or tooling mandates. A material missing criterion or ambiguous required outcome goes to its owning planner/specification/policy stage and leaves the affected check Not assessed. Keep proposed corrections in this report rather than silently rewriting upstream plans.

## 2. Validate the upstream evidence chain

Confirm the specification is **Ready for engineering** and **Current**, implementation plan is **Ready for implementation** with its matching full independent review, test analysis is **Ready for test design**, and the test plan is **Ready for test implementation** with its matching design review. Code, test, and security reviews must each have a full **PASS** and **Current** currency for the applicable final candidate boundary; a self-check or limited review cannot fill a required independent gate.

Read findings, evidence limits, and reviewed versions rather than trusting status labels. Reconcile security, specification and plan conformity, test coverage dispositions, retained/replaced checks, and any accepted nonblocking follow-up. Missing or stale reviews need the owning reviewer's revalidation; the gate cannot extend their scope, close their findings, or convert its own execution into independent review. Expected implementation changes relative to a planning baseline do not alone invalidate a plan; changed contracts, assumptions, requirements, or material design decisions require owner revalidation.

Check differences between each reviewed snapshot and the candidate, including dependencies, fixtures, build/runner settings, configuration, and deployment exposure. Only reuse evidence with a demonstrated match or recorded owner-verified revalidation. No open Blocking/Major finding, failed mandatory condition, or unresolved material deviation may be hidden as post-release work. A valid no-new-test disposition still requires the prescribed existing-check/inspection evidence and applicable regression suites.

The gate operator need not be another independent reviewer unless policy requires it; independence is required where the upstream reviews require it. Do not spawn agents unless the caller requests delegation. Missing authority, access, independence, or evidence leaves dependent checks Not assessed while useful checks continue.

## 3. Execute tests, build, and remaining checks

After establishing identity and grounded commands, prioritize required test execution; respect build/setup dependencies. All required tests and suites must pass for the candidate in their required environments. Inspect discovery/selection and actual assertions/coverage handoffs where needed to confirm the intended suites ran. A green job with zero discovered tests, missing shards, narrowed filters, unexpected skips, disabled cases, or missing required environments is insufficient. A project-established exclusion may be honored only when its authority and preserved required outcomes are evidenced; it cannot waive the all-required-tests condition.

Preserve first-attempt failures, retries, and flaky-test evidence. Do not rerun until green or treat an unexplained retry-only pass as readiness. Diagnose failures as product, test, environment, or unresolved with evidence. A justified transient failure can be cleared by a documented cause, required corrective evidence, and a successful valid run under established policy; otherwise the affected condition remains failed or unassessed. Do not invent retry tolerances or quarantine cases yourself.

Run the applicable production build, type checking, linting, static analysis, security checks, acceptance/regression checks, and established non-functional measurements from the register. Inspect commands for auto-fix, update-baseline, deployment, or other mutation behavior before execution. Build and test outputs may be created in the designated verification workspace; do not alter submitted source, tests, lockfiles, configuration, baselines, dependencies, or checks to obtain PASS. Relevant failures remain visible even if later checks succeed. Continue useful independent checks after failures where execution remains meaningful.

Trusted CI results may satisfy a check without duplicate execution only when their run/job identity, exact candidate/artifact, command/configuration, environment, complete result, and required freshness are inspectable and match the registered condition. Treat cached/reused evidence with the same identity and configuration requirements. Record which checks ran now and which were reused, with reasons. Never transfer passing evidence to a different candidate by assumption.

For security, verify the report's applicable controls, scan scope/configuration, dependency/advisory evidence, and required freshness. Refresh time-sensitive checks where required; route new vulnerabilities or exposure changes to review-security. Scanner success alone cannot replace that review. Confirm established quality/performance/accessibility/compatibility budgets under their specified workload, environment, statistic, and comparison baseline; averages or broad coverage percentages do not replace required outcomes.

For every actual check capture command/method and tool version where material, source/artifact identity, environment, start/end or result date, selection/counts where relevant, exit status/results, artifact/log locator, and evidence limits. Separate Not run/proposed checks from executed results. Redact secrets and personal data; do not upload private code or use external targets beyond existing authorization. If a required action exceeds authority or access, record the exact unblock action and continue independent work.

## 4. Verify delivery and operational readiness

Assess the applicable deployment prerequisites, configuration/secrets availability, target platform compatibility, migrations and mixed-version operation, backup/recovery needs, and rollback or forward-recovery path. A rollback command is insufficient when a migration destroys compatibility or data. Require rehearsal, restore, or staging evidence only when required by policy, risk-grounded plan conditions, or the specification; record missing required execution as Not assessed. Readiness inspection does not authorize production changes.

For deployed services, establish release ownership, relevant health signals/logging/alerts, rollout stages where applicable, concrete stop/rollback conditions, recovery ownership, and the post-deployment smoke/health checks that the deployment owner will run. For packages, desktop apps, static artifacts, or other products, assess the actual distribution/update/recovery model and justify inapplicable service checks. Pre-release evidence cannot establish future production health.

Reconcile all applicable acceptance and Definition of Done outcomes with their actual evidence, including documentation, release notes, migration/operator instructions, and mandated approvals. Link the existing reviewer reconciliations, but inspect unresolved delivery outcomes and contradictions directly. Optional future improvements are not mandatory metrics. Record required approval identity, scope, and exact candidate applicability; do not create an approval step unless the assignment or existing policy requires it.

## 5. Report, route corrections, and revalidate

Use [the report template](assets/release-verification-template.md). Save beside the implementation plan unless the assignment specifies another destination. Inspect an existing report to preserve assignment identity, finding IDs, rounds, and historical candidate-bound verdicts. Follow assignment conventions; otherwise use `RG-001` for gate findings. Preserve upstream IDs and link existing findings rather than duplicating their authority. Write only the gate report and verification evidence in its designated location; remove output placeholders.

Each actionable finding needs ID, severity, failed condition, exact authority/result evidence, candidate identity, affected upstream IDs, consequence, correction owner, and observable closure condition. Use **Blocking** for failed mandatory gates or unresolved material authority/choices, **Major** for material implementation/test/security/delivery defects, and **Minor** for bounded follow-up that cannot change a mandatory result or required behavior. Missing evidence is an unassessed condition with an owner and exact unblock action, not an invented defect. Record confidence/claim status as Observed, Supported, Reported, Inferred, or Unknown where needed to distinguish evidence from claims.

Route code/build/configuration defects to their author and review-code; assertion/discovery issues to the test author and review-tests; security issues to review-security and the responsible author; design/task/criteria gaps to implementation and its reviewer; coverage gaps to test-analysis/design; specification ambiguity to its owning stage; operational and approval prerequisites to their actual owners. The gate does not fix code, change thresholds, waive requirements, or rewrite another review's verdict. Any permitted nonblocking risk needs the required authority, rationale, scope, and expiry/review conditions; relabeling a failed mandatory check cannot make it pass.

On correction compare exact candidate/source changes and author responses. Obtain owner revalidation for affected reviews and rerun affected checks and dependencies; do not validate only the previously failing command if its fix changes wider behavior. Reuse genuinely unaffected evidence only with recorded reasons and limits. Recheck the pinned candidate identity at completion; report/evidence files may change without changing the released payload when explicitly outside that payload boundary. An accidental source/configuration/artifact change requires a new identified candidate and affected revalidation.

Maintain one attempt history across rounds/sessions. After two consecutive unsuccessful gate rechecks of the same blocking finding without material progress, stop the affected correction loop and return BLOCKED with attempts, persistent condition, owner, and exact unblock action. Exclude the initial gate and author self-checks. New labels/versions do not reset the count; material progress meets a closure condition or establishes evidence for a different correction. Stop sooner if no useful correction exists, continue independent checks, and resume only after a documented change addresses the blocker.

## 6. Decide release readiness

Use **Pass**, **Fail**, **Not assessed**, or justified **Not applicable** for each rubric/check-register condition. Every applicable mandatory condition must pass; record distinct outcomes when a rubric row contains multiple checks. No weighted score, majority of green jobs, or partial review can establish full readiness.

- **PASS:** full gate for the exact candidate, all applicable mandatory checks pass, required upstream reviews are Current and independently passed, required tests pass with complete discovery/execution evidence, delivery prerequisites and mandated approvals are evidenced, and no open Blocking/Major findings or unresolved material choices remain. Minor follow-up has explicit rationale and authority where required.
- **REVISE:** diagnosed failed checks or defects remain correctable with available authority and evidence; include other missing conditions and their owners.
- **BLOCKED:** missing evidence, decision, environment, access, required review/approval, or a stalled correction prevents completion. Include known failures. A completed limited gate cannot issue full PASS; use REVISE for diagnosed correctable defects, otherwise BLOCKED for the unassessed full gate.

Track **Current** / **Needs revalidation** separately from verdict and readiness. Mark **Ready for release** only for PASS and Current with all mandated approvals established; otherwise **Not ready for release**. Candidate, relevant source/configuration/environment, upstream decision, dependency/advisory, or approval changes invalidate affected conclusions until rechecked. Record exact changed versions/IDs, evidence freshness limits, and required downstream actions; historical PASS remains bound to its candidate.

Return report path/round, immutable candidate identity, verdict/currency, readiness, test/build/security result summary, and exact blockers or deployment-owner handoff. Identify the artifact eligible for release and the next rollout/health-verification actions. PASS is evidence of readiness, not permission to publish, merge, tag, deploy, migrate production, or declare production healthy; those actions require their own existing authorization.
