# Test plan quality rubric

Scale investigation to risk without leaving applicable conditions unchecked. Cite exact sources, record evidence status and limits, and distinguish existing behavior and performed checks from planned changes and intended checks. A no-new-test plan is assessed against the same obligations; justify inapplicable category/case checks rather than leaving them blank.

| Check | Evidence sufficient to pass |
| --- | --- |
| Authority, baseline, and independence | The exact current reviewed spec and ready production plan (including matching full independent PASS), relevant repository snapshot and local changes, test plan and linked versions, role separation, and any mandated approvals are evidenced. Discrepancies do not remain material. |
| Specification fidelity | Every current required outcome, material AC and applicable DoD, including preserved/removed behavior, has an expected result or evaluation basis grounded in authority and a verification disposition. Assertions would fail on a material violation; no case invents behavior. Justify exclusions from the authoritative scope; do not waive required outcomes. |
| Planned implementation-path adequacy | Separate reconciliation covers material planned logic, interfaces/contracts, data/state changes, and regression-sensitive paths/consumers. For each, the test path and observation (including what is real versus simulated) would catch the cited failure/bypass, or an evidenced inspection, indirect coverage, or no-separate-check rationale is sufficient. Task IDs alone don't establish path coverage. |
| Evidence, risks, and sufficiency | Existing checks' actual assertions, setup, mocks, and selection are inspected where relied upon; execution evidence is explicit. Area classifications, no-new-test decisions, and material risks/limits are justified, with concrete checks or inspections and their evaluation basis. Missing access cannot establish sufficiency. |
| Design and traceability | Selected types have independent-of-each-other specialist assignments with completed, inspected outputs; omissions and overlap are justified. Cases/tasks reverse-trace to a sourced obligation or credible change risk. Replacements and deduplication preserve required assertions and retain supersession links. |
| Observable and implementable | Cases identify meaningful partitions, deterministic inputs/resources, real or simulated boundaries, and assertions with appropriate observation. Tasks are actionable and sequenced against the production plan, assets and tooling, and distinguish design blockers from bounded later setup; proposed work is not reported as completed. |
| Closure and currency | Applicable checks and prior Blocking/Major findings are independently verified on the stated baseline, no material choice remains, and any nonblocking follow-up has a supported disposition. |
