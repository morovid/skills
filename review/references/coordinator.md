# Shared category coordinator procedure

You own the independent category assessment, not the implementation. Use the assigned procedure, rubric, and template. Delegate separable discovery and bulk reads to `morovid-scout` and clerical edits to `morovid-scribe`; request established check runs from the lead, who assigns `morovid-runner`. If you cannot spawn, send bounded assignments to the lead for relay; do the work yourself when no suitable helper is available. You own substantive inspection, verification, closure, and the saved verdict. Only the lead asks the requester questions.

## Establish authority and split the work

- Use the shared baseline from the lead; do not rediscover it for each category. Check applicability of the current **Ready for engineering** specification and **Ready for implementation** code plan with matching full independent **PASS**, plus your category's prerequisites. Require approvals only where mandated. Expected implementation edits do not alone invalidate plans; material changes to behavior, contracts, design commitments, or trust assumptions require owner revalidation. Do not infer missing intent.
- Split bounded tasks by path, dependency, or check where useful; do not create an assignment for every row. Target changed paths, closure criteria, and affected regressions in rechecks. Reuse inspectable exact-snapshot evidence where command, scope, environment, and currency match. Request missing authorized runs through the lead's shared run list before starting them; keep the snapshot unchanged. Assess the implications for your own category.
- Give workers the question and rubric IDs, exact snapshot and authority locators, scope and exclusions, allowed tools/commands and mutation limits, and expected return. Require concise path/line/symbol or artifact locators, affected upstream IDs, candidate finding/check outcome, status (**Observed**, **Supported**, **Reported**, **Inferred**, or **Unknown**), realistic consequence, gaps/conflicts, and read vs. executed evidence. For execution record actual command, snapshot/local changes, environment, tool/configuration where material, selection, result and limits; distinguish proposed or not-run checks. Do not pass off a worker summary as source evidence.

## Verify findings and decide

- Reconcile overlaps and direct follow-ups to workers as needed. Verify material claims, evidence gaps, contradictions, and proposed closures against precise authoritative source, code/test locators or actual run artifacts. Don't accept a worker's conclusion by assertion, or assume absence of a finding means coverage. You own the condition results, finding severity, independent verdict, and closure. Record the identities and scope of workers and editors separately.
- Use **Pass**, **Fail**, **Not assessed**, or source-justified **Not applicable** for every rubric condition and applicable assignment condition; split rows when outcomes differ. Every applicable condition is mandatory for a full gate. Missing evidence, authority, access, currency or independence leaves dependent checks **Not assessed**, not an invented defect. Continue useful independent checks and document uninspected boundaries.
- Preserve upstream IDs, existing finding IDs, and supersession links. Follow assignment conventions, otherwise use the category prefix. Each actionable finding includes severity, failed condition, precise evidence and claim status/limits, affected IDs, realistic consequence, owner, requested correction, and observable closure condition. Default to **Blocking** for failed mandatory gates or unresolved material authority/choices, **Major** for material defects, and **Minor** for bounded follow-up that cannot affect mandatory conditions or required outcomes. Avoid preference-only findings. Cross-link findings routed to other categories or owners; don't rewrite their authority or close their findings.
- **PASS:** full independent assessment on the exact category baseline, all applicable conditions pass and non-applicability is justified, required verification evidence exists, and no open Blocking/Major findings or material discrepancies remain. Minor follow-up needs a rationale and authority where required. **REVISE:** diagnosed defects or discrepancies remain correctable with available authority and evidence. **BLOCKED:** a missing prerequisite, independence, access, evidence, or stalled correction prevents completion; include known defects. For an intentionally limited assessment with no other blocker, use REVISE for diagnosed correctable defects or otherwise BLOCKED for the unassessed full gate. Track **Current** / **Needs revalidation** separately; old PASS results are bound to their reviewed versions.

## Save and recheck

- Delegate separable mechanical report edits to `morovid-scribe` with verified content and an exclusive path; the coordinator can write directly when that would only add a relay or no worker is available. An editor does not adjudicate checks or rewrite verdicts. Verify the saved report; remove placeholders and redact sensitive data. Do not edit the implementation, tests, configuration, baselines, upstream authority, or sibling reports. If a report cannot be saved and verified, return evidence and the blocker, not a completed PASS.
- On resubmission start from the project diff since the reviewed fingerprint, then verify closure against exact changed versions and author responses, affected dependencies, and regressions. Broaden inspection if the change boundary is unclear; reuse unchanged evidence only with recorded reasons and limits. An author's claim is not closure. Reopen when warranted and record affected versions/IDs and downstream revalidation.
- [Keep the report to the current round](artifact-repository.md#keep-reports-to-the-current-round): open findings in full with their attempt counters, findings closed this round as one line each, and earlier rounds as one line each. Delete earlier closed findings and earlier rounds' details; read them with the [history commands](artifact-repository.md#reading-history) when needed. After two consecutive unsuccessful independent rechecks of the same blocking finding without material progress, stop that loop and return BLOCKED with attempts, persistent condition, owner, and exact unblock action. Exclude initial review and author self-checks. Renaming or a new version does not reset the count; progress meets a closure condition or establishes evidence for a different correction. Stop sooner when no useful correction is possible; continue independent work and resume only after a documented change addresses the blocker.
- Return report path/round ("Round N of 5"), snapshot and inspection boundary, verdict/currency, separate category conclusions, verification status/limits, and next owner/action to the lead. If this is round 5 and the verdict is not **PASS**, say that the round limit is reached and the lead must escalate to a human.
